Training Phases — Aug 2026 to May 2027
Each phase builds on the last. Don’t skip ahead.
PHASE 1 — AUG 2 → SEP 12
Build the Habit
6 weeks. Show up. The goal is consistency, not intensity.
› 50 min sessions, 5 days/week
› Moderate weight — learn movements, perfect form
› 3-4 sets, 10-12 reps per exercise
› Walking pad 90 min/day every workday
› 200g protein daily — non-negotiable
› Win condition: 5 sessions/week for 6 straight weeks
PHASE 2 — SEP 13 → OCT 24
Progressive Overload
6 weeks. Add weight every 1-2 weeks. Sessions get harder naturally.
› Same movements, heavier weight each week
› Bump to 4-5 sets per exercise
› Sessions reach 60 min — normal and expected
› Add incline on pad post-lift for extra burn
› Track weights every session in a notebook
› Win condition: every lift heavier than 6 weeks ago
PHASE 3 — OCT 25 → DEC 5
Volume Increase
6 weeks. More exercises, supersets, 60-70 min sessions.
› Add a second exercise per major muscle group
› Introduce supersets to keep intensity high
› Drop sets on final set of each exercise
› Bump pad speed slightly on post-lift walk
› Win condition: 215 lbs or below by Dec 5
PHASE 4 — DEC 6 → MAR 1
Strength + Knee Rebuild
12 weeks. Sports medicine consult. Potentially unlock controlled leg work.
› Book sports medicine appointment — Dec 6 target
› Add goblet squats or leg press if cleared
› Lower rep ranges on key lifts (6-8 reps, heavier)
› Add 2x/week dedicated core sessions
› Win condition: 205 lbs by Mar 1
PHASE 5 — MAR 1 → MAY 12
Finishing Phase
10 weeks to deadline. Final push to 195.
› 6 training days/week if body is recovered
› Tighten calories, keep protein at 200g
› Full lower body if knee is cleared
› Win condition: 195 lbs on the scale May 12, 2027
