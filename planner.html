The Full Call Sequence
COLD CALL
Earn a scheduled conversation only
No model, no comp, no structure. Lead with: more business than Advisor capacity, built a pro forma with your numbers, worth 20 minutes. End with specific date and time booked.
CALL 1 — YOU OWN THIS
Qualify. Surface income goals. Close for Call 2.
Five stages: (1) Opener + rapport (2) Discovery — candidate talks 70%, surface setup/production/constraint (3) Model intro — 3 points only, no math yet (4) Income goals — MANDATORY before any numbers (5) Close for Call 2 — logical next step, not a decision ask.
CALL 2
Walk the pro forma. Remove execution risk.
Operations and leadership call. Show the math now that income goals are established. Closing step: schedule Call 3 with Mike and Dave.
CALL 3 — MIKE + DAVE
Peer validation only.
Mike and Dave speak candidly from the Advisor seat. End with one question: "What would need to be true for you to feel comfortable moving forward?" Their answer is the Call 4 roadmap.
CALL 4
Decision conversation — not more pitching.
Built entirely around what candidate said at Call 3. Name the decision, surface the blocker. End with yes, hard no, or specific date with real reason. No open-ended nurture after Call 4.
The Income Goal Gate — Non-Negotiable
Your #1 failure pattern: pitching before income goals are locked. This gate is the fix.
HARD RULE: NO NUMBER = NO ADVANCE. EVERY TIME.
  • →Gate question: "Before I walk you through what this looks like, I want to make sure it actually makes sense for you — what are you currently earning, and where do you need to be in the next 12 months?"
  • →Always clarify gross vs take-home. A candidate saying $200K gross vs $200K take-home changes the entire math conversation.
  • →Never suggest a number. If they ask what others make: "That depends entirely on where you're starting from — that's why I want to understand your situation first."
  • →Minimum handoff package for Call 2: (1) Current income gross + take-home (2) Target income — their number (3) Timeline (4) Stated constraint or reason for considering a move.
  • →Skeptical candidates are more valuable than excited ones. Skepticism signals real evaluation. Treat it as information, not resistance.
